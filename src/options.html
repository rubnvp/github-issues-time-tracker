Settings

Choose how you track your time.

Usage level

Each level includes the features of the previous one.

Higher-level features pause when you switch to a lower level. Your settings and recorded time are kept.

Picture-in-Picture

Daily focus goal

The goal is the same every day. Reduced-motion settings disable the bar animation.

With work hours set, the white marker shows your expected pace. When paused, green means on track; amber means behind.

Focus reminders

Reminders need an open PiP and an enabled focus goal. They activate when you're paused and behind pace during work hours, not during breaks.

Work schedule

Set work hours for each day in your local time. Add intervals to leave breaks; split overnight shifts across days. PiP shows the day's progress, excluding breaks and independently of focus time.
