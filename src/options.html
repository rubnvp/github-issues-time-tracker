Picture-in-Picture

Daily focus goal

The goal is the same every day. Reduced-motion settings disable the bar animation.

If pace comparison is enabled, the white marker shows your expected pace. When paused, green means on track; amber means behind.

Focus pace

Focus reminders

Reminders need an open PiP, an enabled focus goal and pace comparison. They activate when you're paused and behind pace during work hours, not during breaks.

Enable pace comparison to use reminders. Your reminder settings are saved.

Work schedule

Set work hours for each day in your local time. Add intervals to leave breaks; split overnight shifts across days. PiP shows the day's progress, excluding breaks and independently of focus time.
