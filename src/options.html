Daily focus goal

Choose pace indicators and reminders in Pace.

Focus pace

Pace assumes focus is spread evenly across work hours. Enable a focus goal and set work hours in Workday to compare.

Focus reminders

Reminders work independently of the indicators. They require an open PiP and a focus goal, and activate when paused and behind during work hours.

Work schedule

Set work hours for each day in your local time. Add intervals to leave breaks; split overnight shifts across days. PiP shows the day's progress, excluding breaks and independently of focus time.

Replaces existing hours from Tuesday through Sunday, including days off.
